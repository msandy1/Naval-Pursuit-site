How it works

Deploy. Fire. Deduce.

The classic paper game is a one-on-one duel. Here, everyone shares the same water, so every shot fired might hit anyone, including you.

01

Deploy

Place your five ships horizontally, vertically or diagonally while friends are still joining, or let the game auto-deploy them for you. Ready up, and the host starts the battle.

02

Fire

You get one shot per ship still afloat. Lose ships, lose firepower. Mark targets during other players' turns so your volley is ready the moment yours begins.

03

Deduce

The board only shows the round each shot landed in. Each player's tracker shows the rounds their ships were hit. Match the two, and the fleets reveal themselves.

Features

Built for game night

Shared battlefield

Up to 11 players on one grid, in three sizes: 10×10, 13×13 and 15×15. Alliances form; betrayals follow.

Missiles and sonar

Your volley launches with missile trails while everyone else sees streaks drop in. Then a sonar sweep loads each round number with a ping.

Quick comms

Trash talk and treaties in one tap: pick a ready-made line, no typing and nothing to moderate.

Plays anywhere

A command-console layout on a computer and a thumb-friendly one on your phone. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Your-turn alerts

No need to finish in one sitting. Turn on notifications and the game tells you when it's your move, so a battle can run all day.

No sign-up

Pick a callsign and you're in. Invite friends with a four-letter code or a link, with no accounts or passwords to remember.

FAQ

Before you set sail

Do I need to download anything?

No. Naval Pursuit runs in any modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your home screen if you'd like it to open like an app.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose a callsign when you create or join a sector, and that's it.

How many people can play?

Two to eleven commanders per sector. It's at its best with a crowd, when every shot could hit anyone.

How do I invite friends?

Create a sector and send your friends its four-letter code or the invite link. They join straight from their own device.

Do we have to play at the same time?

Not at all. Play in one quick session around the table, or take turns throughout the day with your-turn notifications.

Is there an Android app?

An Android app is coming soon. Until then, the browser version plays great on Android phones.

Ready to take command?

Open a sector, send the code to your friends, and find out who's left afloat.
